About ROC-Man

ROC-Man is an independent rock artist, songwriter and multi-instrumentalist from Germany.

His music combines powerful rock elements with emotional melodies, cinematic atmosphere and a strong sense of storytelling.

After decades of experience with bass, guitar, keyboards and vocals, ROC-Man writes, arranges and produces his own music with a focus on individuality, emotion and musical variety.

Style Rock · Cinematic · Emotional
