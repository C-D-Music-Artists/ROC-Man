VAELIRA & CYRAVON

An Aelovari Encounter

Two luminous creatures. One unexpected encounter on K2-18b.

Deep within the forests of K2-18b, something moves between the ancient trees.

From the shimmering vegetation, Vaelira appears.

Moments later, a second creature emerges — Cyravon.

They do not flee.
They do not attack.

For a moment, two mysterious beings meet in the silence of the forest.

Then the Aelovari disappear between the trees as silently as they came.

Yet something remains — the feeling that their story has only just begun.

This was not their last encounter.

Meet the Aelovari

Aelovari are luminous flying creatures native to K2-18b. Small, graceful and rarely seen, they move through the alien forests on four to six wings.

SpeciesAelovari
Size15–30 cm
Wings4–6
HabitatK2-18b

Vaelira

Female · Azure Aelovari

Pronunciation: Vae-li-ra

Meaning in the language of the Aeloni:

“The turquoise light that dances across the water.”

Vaelira glows in shades of turquoise and violet.

Cyravon

Male · Crimson Aelovari

Pronunciation: Ky-ra-won

Meaning in the language of the Aeloni:

“The warm light that follows the red wind.”

Cyravon carries the colours of ruby, copper and gold.
