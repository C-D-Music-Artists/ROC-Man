Chapter 1

What Was Missing

Nevio di Grande lived a life many people envied. He loved beautiful things, fast cars, and the feeling of having achieved something. From the outside, it seemed as if nothing was missing.

But in the quiet moments, he could feel it.

There was something success could not replace.

He just did not yet know what he was really searching for.

“I wanna be the best man of the world …”

Chapter 2

The Cellist

Then he heard her.

Not her voice – her cello.

Giulia Mirabella stood on the stage of a concert hall. In her mid-thirties, confident and completely absorbed in her music.

As she played, the world around her seemed to stand still for a moment.

Nevio could not take his eyes off her.

Chapter 3

The Encounter

After the concert, they met.

A few words became a conversation. A conversation became an evening together.

Giulia talked about music, dreams, and how the best moments in life are often the ones that cannot be planned.

Nevio listened.

For the first time, he did not want to be the best. He wanted to be a better man.

“I wanna be a better man for you …”

Chapter 4

What Really Matters

In the days that followed, Giulia showed him her world.

Music. Small cafés. Streets at night. Encounters with people Nevio might once have simply passed by.

He began to understand that a person could own many things and still be searching for something.

Maybe being rich meant something completely different.

“I will feel good. I will be rich.”

Chapter 5

Be Rich

It was late, and outside it had begun to rain.

Nevio and Giulia were still sitting together. Behind the window, the lights of the city blurred in the rain.

No large audience. No perfect stage. No applause.

Just two people and a moment that belonged to them.

Nevio looked at Giulia.

Maybe this was what it meant to be rich.

And maybe this was only the beginning of their story.

“Be rich tonight.”
