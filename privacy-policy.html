2. General Information

We take the protection of personal data seriously. Personal data is processed only to the extent necessary for providing and operating this website and in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, in particular the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub.

When this website is accessed, technical data may be processed by GitHub as part of providing and securing the hosting service. This may include the visitor's IP address and other technical connection information.

This processing is necessary for the technical delivery, security and stability of the website.

4. Server Log Data

When the website is accessed, the hosting infrastructure may automatically process technical connection and access data.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Requested page or resource
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer information

This technical processing serves purposes such as website delivery, security, stability and the prevention of misuse.

5. Local Fonts and Icons

The fonts and icons used on this website are hosted locally as part of the website.

No connection to Google Fonts, Font Awesome content delivery networks or other external font or icon providers is established for the delivery of these resources.

6. External Links and Social Media

This website contains links to external platforms, including Spotify, YouTube and Facebook.

Content from these platforms is not embedded directly into this website. In particular, no YouTube video player or Spotify player is loaded automatically when visiting this website.

A connection to these external platforms is generally established only when a visitor actively follows the corresponding link.

After following an external link, the privacy policies and data processing practices of the respective platform apply.

7. Contact by Email

If you contact ROC-Man or C.D. Music Artists by email, the information you provide will be processed for the purpose of handling and responding to your enquiry.

Depending on the nature of the enquiry, this may include your email address, name and any other information contained in your message.

The information will not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

8. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not currently use its own analytics, advertising tracking or marketing cookies.

Spotify, YouTube and Facebook content is not embedded directly into the website. These services are accessed only when a visitor actively follows the corresponding external link.

If analytics, embedded media, advertising technologies or other services requiring consent are introduced in the future, this privacy policy will be updated and an appropriate consent mechanism will be implemented where legally required.

9. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, data subjects may have various rights regarding the processing of their personal data. These include, where the applicable legal requirements are met:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

10. Data Security

Appropriate technical and organisational measures are used where applicable to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or misuse.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the website, its technical infrastructure or the services used on it change.
